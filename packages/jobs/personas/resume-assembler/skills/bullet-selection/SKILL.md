---
id: bullet-selection
name: Bullet selection
description: How to choose approved bullets for a posting without inventing content.
---
- Match on the posting's stated technologies and responsibilities, not on seniority flattery.
- Prefer bullets whose tags appear in the posting's title or requirements.
- If two bullets cover the same ground, keep the one with the more specific outcome.
- An empty slot is acceptable; a fabricated bullet is not.
